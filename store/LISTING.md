Working name: **Streak Maintaining App** (matches the manifest).
Suggested category: Productivity. Language: English.

## Short description

Build learning streaks from supported GeeksforGeeks articles, save your place, and get optional reminders.

## Detailed description

Keep a daily learning routine with clear goals, saved Continue links and a calendar
of completed days. Create several goals, choose your daily lesson or minute target,
and connect one goal at a time to supported free GeeksforGeeks Python/DSA articles.

The extension observes foreground reading time and coverage on those connected
articles. A new qualifying article can complete your daily goal automatically.
Skipping straight to the bottom does not meet the reading rule. This is **Browser
tracked activity**, not independent proof of learning, and it can be fabricated by
a determined device owner. Videos, quizzes and coding submissions are unsupported.

Use Continue to reopen a saved article and restore an observed position. Opening a
page or restoring position never awards skipped reading credit. Keep multiple goals,
see current and best streaks, earn local prototype XP and one-time milestone bonuses,
and inspect History and Rewards. A missed day resets the current streak; past best
streaks and earned rewards remain.

Optional daily and contextual notifications can help you return to unfinished work.
Notifications need your permission, respect quiet hours and can be affected by
browser/OS settings. Contextual nudges use the tracked source session; they do not
store the website you switched to.

Your bookmarks, reading checkpoints and prototype progress stay on this browser.
Optional account/cloud features require a configured service: email-code sign-in,
explicit goal-definition copying and opt-in sync of newly started articles. Cloud
records are shown separately and use the server receipt day. Earlier local XP is
not imported; unconfirmed submissions expire after seven days.

The extension handles relevant article URLs/titles, observed reading activity and
saved position to provide tracking and Continue. It checks open tabs to reuse a
matching saved lesson, rather than opening duplicates. It does not build a general
browsing-history archive. It reads article text in memory to measure coverage;
raw article prose is not stored or uploaded by the app. Optional cloud sync sends
the canonical article path and bounded reading evidence to your account.

Disconnect tracking, export your data, or clear device activity in Settings. A
technical diagnostic export is optional and shared manually; no automatic analytics
or feedback upload is enabled. The extension is independent of GeeksforGeeks and
does not claim its approval or affiliation.
